## Referenced Frameworks & Standards

### ITIL v4
- **Full name:** Information Technology Infrastructure Library, version 4
- **Owner:** PeopleCert (acquired AXELOS in 2021)
- **Copyright status:** Commercially proprietary — All Rights Reserved
- **License for reproduction:** None granted. PeopleCert permits referencing and non-commercial academic commentary; does not permit verbatim reproduction of practice descriptions, diagrams, or structural frameworks.
- **How GaaS uses it:** GaaS draws on ITIL v4 service management *concepts* (service value chain, incident/change/problem management disciplines, continual improvement) expressed entirely in original GaaS language with original diagrams. No ITIL text is reproduced verbatim.
- **Legal basis:** The *idea-expression dichotomy* (Baker v. Selden; 17 U.S.C. § 102(b)) — concepts and methodologies are not copyrightable, only their specific expression is.
- **Attribution:** ITIL® is a registered trademark of PeopleCert. GaaS is not affiliated with or endorsed by PeopleCert or AXELOS.
- **Source:** [axelos.com/certifications/itil-service-management](https://www.axelos.com/certifications/itil-service-management)

### TBM Framework (Technology Business Management)
- **Full name:** Technology Business Management Taxonomy and Framework
- **Owner:** TBM Council (industry nonprofit)
- **Copyright status:** Publications marked All Rights Reserved; however, the TBM Council explicitly publishes the Taxonomy for free reading and use at tbmcouncil.org
- **US Government adoption:** The TBM framework is the official IT financial management standard for US federal agencies, mandated via OMB guidance and implemented through CIO.gov. Federal government publications using TBM (e.g., OMB Circulars, CIO.gov guidance documents) are US government works and are public domain under 17 U.S.C. § 105.
- **How GaaS uses it:** GaaS references the TBM cost allocation methodology and tower structure as the basis for its fiscal architecture layer, using the TBM Council's own publicly available descriptions and US federal government public domain implementations. No proprietary TBM publications are reproduced verbatim.
- **Attribution:** TBM Framework and TBM Taxonomy are published by the TBM Council. [tbmcouncil.org](https://www.tbmcouncil.org)
- **Source:** [tbmcouncil.org/learn-tbm/resource-center/the-tbm-taxonomy-5/](https://www.tbmcouncil.org/learn-tbm/resource-center/the-tbm-taxonomy-5/)

### COFOG (Classification of the Functions of Government)
- **Full name:** Classification of the Functions of Government
- **Owner:** United Nations Statistics Division
- **Copyright status:** UN copyright — non-sales publication. Per UNSD policy, no permission is required to reproduce excerpts from non-sales UN Statistics Division publications provided proper credits are given.
- **UNdata policy:** "All data and metadata provided on UNdata are available free of charge and may be copied freely, duplicated and further distributed provided that UNdata is cited as the reference."
- **How GaaS uses it:** GaaS uses the COFOG division and group classification structure as the basis for mapping government service portfolios. The classification structure is reproduced with attribution.
- **Attribution:** COFOG — Classification of the Functions of Government. United Nations Statistics Division, Statistical Papers Series M No. 84, Rev. 1 (2000). Available at: [unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/cofog](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/cofog)

### Zero Trust Architecture
- **Full name:** Zero Trust Architecture (ZTA)
- **Owner:** NIST (National Institute of Standards and Technology, US Department of Commerce)
- **Copyright status:** US federal government work — **public domain** under 17 U.S.C. § 105
- **Key publication:** NIST SP 800-207 *Zero Trust Architecture* (2020)
- **How GaaS uses it:** GaaS adopts ZTA principles as documented in NIST SP 800-207 and CISA guidance. These are public domain and may be reproduced freely with attribution.
- **Attribution:** NIST SP 800-207, *Zero Trust Architecture*. National Institute of Standards and Technology, U.S. Department of Commerce (2020). [doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-207](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-207)
